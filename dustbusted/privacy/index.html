Privacy policy

We (Happy Bao Studios) don't collect or store any personal data ourselves. The game shows ads through Google AdMob (on Android, and on iPhone from version 1.1; iPhone version 1.0 has no ads), and Google collects some data to do that, as described below.

  • There is no account to create and no analytics of our own.
  • Your progress (scores, unlocks, settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes it.
  • On iPhone, if you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your daily challenge and Endless scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we never see your Apple ID. You can turn Game Center off in the Settings app.

Ads

The game shows a short ad between runs (from version 1.2, and not in your first runs), and offers optional ads for a bonus (getting back up once per run, doubling the buttons from a run, or another try at the daily challenge) that you can always say no to. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking when your iPhone asks; on Android, your device's advertising ID) and an identifier for this app;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and how the app performs (for example crashes and loading times).

Ads may be personalised to your interests if you allow it; if you don't, you still see ads, just not personalised ones. On iPhone you can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android, reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads). Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first, and can change their choice later with Ad privacy choices on the title screen.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

The game is rated 9+ and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG or lower).

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
