Privacy policy

Dustbusted! does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • There is no account to create, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Your progress (scores, unlocks, settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes it.
  • If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your daily challenge and Endless scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we never see your Apple ID. You can turn Game Center off in the Settings app.
  • The game works offline and does not track you across other apps or websites.

The game is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect information from children, or from anyone else.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
