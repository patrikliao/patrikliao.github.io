Game Dev Factory: Privacy Policy

Happy Bao Studios · Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version

Game Dev Factory does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved game (your studio, games and progress) and your settings (sound, accessibility).
  • If you switch on Playtest mode, a log of how you play and any feedback notes you write. This log never leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
