Pocket Sanctuary

The virtual pet you remember, all grown up.

Hatch it. Feed it. Clean up after it.

Every egg is a mystery. Mix an animal, a field and a charm, and the time of day, the weather and each animal's secret favourites decide what hatches. Look after it well and it grows from a baby into an adult, with a personality shaped by how you raised it.

What's inside

128 pets to discover

16 animals from four regions, each with 8 variants, from Frost to the rare Aurora.

Real pet care

Feeding, fetch, naps and a proper bath with soap, shower and towel. Yes, there's poo.

Silly outfits

Hats, scarves, capes and wings, plus camera photos with stickers to match.

Battles and adventures

Themed arenas with champions, balanced skill matches, and battles against your friends' teams.

Grows while you're away

Your pet keeps growing when the app is closed, and a daily gift waits when you come back.

Keep every pet

Grown-ups move into your Sanctuary, and every pet you raise stays in your Collection forever.
